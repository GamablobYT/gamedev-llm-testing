# Tidal Bell — visual brief

## Explored directions

| Direction | Overall concept and mood | Palette and material | Creature and environment forms | Light, VFX, emotional effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tidal Bell** | An abandoned tidal observatory, hushed and uncanny | Pearl, deep petrol blue, sea glass and a small coral accent; glazed shell, dark wet stone and luminous inlay | A suspended bell shell, long hinged fin arms and small probing feet; concentric plinths and tuning forks | Grazing cyan moonlight, a warm core, restrained rings and droplets; awe before a living instrument |
| **Paper Eclipse** | A violent ceremonial paper theater | Ivory, vermilion and charcoal; layered fibrous paper and ink | Folded blade limbs and fans; giant flat screens and sliced negative space | Hard graphic shadow, torn edges and ink streaks; tension and precision |
| **Glass Orchard** | A crystalline creature in a geometric greenhouse | Jade, lilac and pale gold; translucent mineral, enamel and brass | Branching antlers and split hooves; trellises and faceted fruit | Refracted daylight and angular glints; alluring danger |

## Chosen direction

**Tidal Bell** offers the strongest connection between anatomy, attack language, and arena. The shell is both silhouette and phase signal. Fin arms visibly prepare the sweep and pierce. Circular architecture supports the pulse and provides distance markers. The combat palette reserves coral for danger and bright pearl for player actions so tells remain readable over the blue field.

## Encounter beats

1. Approach the dormant creature. The shell opens and the fight begins.
2. Read and answer three distinct attacks: a broad fin sweep, a narrow impaling thrust, and an expanding resonance ring.
3. At half health the shell splits wide, the arena's tuning channels energize, and attacks acquire delayed echoes.
4. Defeat collapses the creature into the observatory's central pool. Death permits immediate restart.
